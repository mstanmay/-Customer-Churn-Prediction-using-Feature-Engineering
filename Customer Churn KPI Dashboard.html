KPI definitions. Churn rate = churned / total customers. Retention rate = 1 − churn rate. Revenue lost = monthly charges of churned customers. Revenue at risk = monthly charges of active customers on Month-to-Month contracts or with 3+ support tickets. Tenure bands: 0–12, 13–24, 25+ months. Sample size is small (15 customers), so treat percentages as illustrative.
